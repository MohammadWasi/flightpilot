# Code of Conduct

FlightPilot welcomes people of every background and experience level. Participants are expected to make collaboration respectful, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code respectfully, and give specific, actionable feedback.
- Be patient with questions, accept corrections, and respect other people's time and boundaries.
- Credit others' work and protect private information, including traveller data and credentials.
- Help keep technical discussions relevant to the project.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable. Neither is publishing someone's private information without permission. Disagreement about technical decisions is welcome; targeting a person is not.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies to the project's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other community spaces, as well as conduct while representing the project.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer, [@MohammadWasi](https://github.com/MohammadWasi), at **mdwasi15@gmail.com**, using the subject `FlightPilot conduct report`. Include links, a description of the behavior, and any immediate concerns. Do not post sensitive evidence in public. Reports are handled as privately as practical; information may be shared with people needed to investigate or act, and absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

If a report concerns the maintainer or you do not feel comfortable contacting them, use [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-community-guidelines#reporting-abuse). The project currently has one maintainer and no independent conduct committee.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews available evidence and may remove content, request a change in behavior, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or ban a participant. The response depends on severity, repetition, and community safety. Threats, harassment, and other serious incidents may warrant immediate restriction.

People affected by an enforcement decision may request reconsideration by emailing the contact above with relevant context. The maintainer may revise a decision when new evidence warrants it. No response-time commitment is implied.
